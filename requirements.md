Mini-World Description

The university department provides equipment to its students including laptops, cameras, projectors, microphones and networking equipment. The proposed database will hold information about the students, equipment, their categories, and their checkout records.
Data to be Stored

The database should store information about the students including the student ID, first and last names, email address, date of birth, and phone numbers. In addition, an equipment item needs to store its ID, name, serial number, purchase date, status and its category. The category must also have an ID, category name, and description. Besides that, the record must store information about the checkout which includes the checkout ID, student ID, equipment ID, checkout date, due date, return date, and returned status.

Users of the Database

The database will be used by the department staff to check equipment in and out. The staff will also monitor the inventory of the equipment. In addition, the database will be used by the university’s administration and faculty to track the borrowing of the equipment.

Business Rules

1. Each equipment item may belong only to one category.
2. One student may borrow several items of equipment.
3. Each checkout record refers only to one student.
4. Each checkout record refers only to one item of equipment.
5. The equipment item may be checked out to only one student at a time.
6. Some students may not borrow any equipment items.

7. One equipment item may be checked out many times.
8. A checkout record might not have a return date if the equipment item has not been returned yet.
9. Any checkout record must have a due date that is after the checkout date.
10. A returned equipment item must have a return condition recorded.
Questions Asked of the Database
1. What students are in the database?
2. What equipment items does the department have?
3. What equipment category does each item belong to?
4. What equipment items has a particular student borrowed?
5. Who has borrowed equipment items?
6. What equipment is currently on file?
7. What equipment items have never been checked out?
8. What items have been returned after their due date?
9. Who has a particular item of equipment?
10. How many checkouts has each student made?
